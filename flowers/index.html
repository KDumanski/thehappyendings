Our story

Flowers that knit a neighborhood back together.

Queens Perennial began in the thick of the pandemic, when founder Helen Ho went looking for a safe way to keep her community connected. What started as a few neighbors gathering for fresh blooms has grown into a seasonal tradition across the city.

Every share is more than a bouquet. A CSA, Community Supported Agriculture, is a promise: a group of people pledge to support a farm for the whole season, sharing in both its bounty and its risk. That up-front support is what lets a small farm plan, invest, and weather the swings of weather and market that small growers face alone.

We pour that support into Luna Family Farm, a farmer-of-color owned and operated farm in central New Jersey, and into the organizers of color who keep our neighborhoods growing.

“How many things in your week are just purely joyful? I look forward to Queens Perennial pick-ups every week.” Lesslie, longtime member

Why our flowers

Grown clean. Picked fresh. Grown for good.

Most supermarket bouquets are flown in from overseas, days old, and doused along the way. Ours are a different thing entirely.

No synthetic pesticides

Grown without synthetic pesticides, the way flowers were meant to be. Better for the soil, the growers, and the table you set them on.

Cut the same morning

Many stems are harvested the very morning you pick them up, so a Queens Perennial bouquet lasts far longer than flowers shipped across the world.

Rooted in real care

Grown on healthy soil by a family farm, not an industrial supplier, and chosen for beauty and season, never for what ships best.

How it works

Four steps to a season of flowers

Pledge once in spring, then collect a fresh hand-tied bouquet every week, all season long.

  1. 01

    Pledge your share

    Choose a season-long or flexible share in spring. Your up-front pledge funds seeds, supplies and a fair living for the farm.

  2. 02

    We grow & gather

    Luna Family Farm grows the flowers; our team hand-ties bouquets and brings them into the city, often picked the very same morning.

  3. 03

    Stroll & pick up

    Swing by your neighborhood café, bar or shop on pickup day and grab that week's bouquet. No two weeks are ever the same.

  4. 04

    Bloom all season

    Fresh flowers on your table every week from June to November, and a small farm that's stronger because of you.

    Our farm partner

    Luna Family Farm

    Every Queens Perennial bouquet is grown by Luna Family Farm, a farmer-of-color owned and operated farm in central New Jersey, run alongside a beloved local bakery.

    “Farmers of color are still a rarity today. It's not for lack of desire from people of color who want to farm, but stems from issues of systemic racism and the distribution of wealth that still exist in society.” Choosing Luna is how our members push, in a small and beautiful way, against that imbalance.

    Pablo Jr. and the Luna family will be your guides through the growing season, from the first tulips of spring to the last ornamental kale of November.

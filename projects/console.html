Concept: The Happy Endings as a 16-bit console

The main screen is the shared synthwave city — stars, retro sun, neon skyline, glowing grid floor — with a little guy running through it. It boots on its own (no "press start"), then lands on the menu: your mission text and the two buttons (Start a Project, Portfolio) docked below the skyline, with a marquee ribbon along the bottom. Portfolio opens a stage-select where the six clients are game levels. Click the screen, then arrow keys + Enter (Esc to back out).

The real synthwave scene from the site is the backdrop; a neon runner sprints across the horizon in front of the sun while the city scrolls and the grid floor flows.

Menu

Mission + two buttons

Your statement and the two options dock at the bottom over a scrim so they stay readable, with the neon ticker under them.
